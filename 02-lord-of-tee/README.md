# Lord of Tee — Brand Identity & Social Media

## Project Type

Brand Identity • Fashion • Social Media Design

## Overview

A visual identity project created for Lord of Tee, exploring premium fashion, traditional influence and modern luxury.

The work focused on building a consistent visual language across the brand, from identity and packaging to clothing and social media applications.

## Creative Direction

The visual direction combines deep navy, warm gold and neutral tones to communicate quality, elegance and timelessness.

The identity was applied across different brand touchpoints to create a cohesive and recognisable presence.

## Deliverables

- Logo and visual identity
- Colour palette
- Social media designs
- Clothing mockups
- Packaging concepts
- Brand applications
