# Lord of Tee — Brand Identity & Social Media

## Project Type

Brand Identity • Fashion • Social Media Design

## Overview

A visual identity concept created for Lord of Tee, exploring premium fashion, traditional influence and modern luxury.

The project included brand identity development and social media visuals designed to create a consistent and recognisable brand presence.

## Creative Direction

The visual direction combines deep navy, warm gold and neutral tones to communicate quality, elegance and timelessness.

The identity was extended across clothing, packaging, accessories and social media applications.

## Deliverables

- Logo and visual identity
- Colour palette
- Social media visuals
- Clothing mockups
- Packaging concepts
- Brand applications
